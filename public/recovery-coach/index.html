How to fill my TV screen without cutting off Flex

Samsung Galaxy Fold: open Smart View in Quick Settings, select your TV, rotate the Fold to landscape and choose the Samsung setting for full screen on the connected device when available. Then tap TV / CAST here. On desktop Chrome, use the browser’s Cast tab/screen option. The app cannot automatically connect to a TV or change Samsung's Smart View settings.
