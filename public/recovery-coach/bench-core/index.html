Bench-supported core workouts

Two optional core circuits you can perform seated on your adjustable weight bench with your injured leg supported. This is a separate guided text-and-timer workout, not a new approved Flex motion video or Marcus recording.

Foot cast · clinician clearance required. Weight-bearing ability does not automatically clear abdominal bracing, resisted anti-rotation or bench exercise. Use a stable, locked bench with secure back support as needed; never push through the cast, use it as an anchor, or place the casted foot under a weight. Do not attempt leg raises, planks, sit-ups, bench crunches or forceful twisting under the current recovery plan. Stop for pain, swelling, dizziness or cast pressure. Have your clinician clear each movement, load and duration first.
