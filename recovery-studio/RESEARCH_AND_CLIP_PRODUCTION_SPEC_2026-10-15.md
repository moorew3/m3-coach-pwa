## Critical safety boundary

Medical advice is not established by fitness tutorials or visual renders. The treating orthopedist must clarify whether EACH proposed arm-only, resisted, or aerobic movement is permitted while strict NWB, including transfers, safe limb elevation, resistance level, effort and duration. If this clearance is missing, keep resistance programs locked and do not describe any AI clip as rehabilitation or safe to imitate. Refer to:
- Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital: https://www.rnoh.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-guides/instructions-patients-leg-cast — do NOT bear weight until directed.
- Frimley NHS: https://www.fhft.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-library/instructions-patients-plaster-casts-fph/submit/7561 — NWB cast, protect heel from direct pressure, elevate as advised.
- NHS Fife: https://www.nhsfife.org/services/all-services/patient-advice/care-of-a-cast-splint-or-surgical-boot/ — ask clinicians before sporting activities in casts.
- South Tees NHS: https://www.southtees.nhs.uk/resources/plaster-casts-and-immobilisation/ — no heavy lifting unless advised.
- VA physiotherapy (different patient group, not permission for this injury): https://veteranshealthlibrary.va.gov/Rehab/PhysicalTherapy/LowerLimbAmputation/3%2C88874 — medically supervised arm conditioning, residual limb fully supported.

Do not infer that 'seated' means safe. Source demonstrations regularly use feet flat on floor. ## Exercise-by-exercise research — Equipment A

### A1 — Seated dumbbell biceps curls
- **Actual exercise video:** Muscle & Strength seated curl: https://cdn.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/seated-dumbbell-curl.html ; **professional form:** ACE https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/44/seated-biceps-curl/
- **Equipment:** photographed fixed hex dumbbells or adjustable dumbbells.
- **Movement:** supported upright seat; palms facing FORWARD/up (supinated), dumbbells hang close to sides, shoulders down. Hinge at ELBOWS to curl weights toward chest, then fully reverse to sides while wrists stay aligned. Upper arms remain near torso; no forward swinging.
- **Critical difference:** normal supinated curl, not neutral-grip hammer curl or side shoulder raise.
- **Reject:** only one arm moves if bilateral rep was requested; forearm twisting into a hammer grip; shrugs, lateral raises, no full descent, torso/leg stabilizing, cast shifts.
- **Status:** original real 8-second Flex video exists in Drive and app; **visual identity approved by user** but video shows ONE-ARM curl. Preserve as a visual sample only rather than falsely accepting bilateral exercise mechanics.
- **Clip script:** 'Original approved Flex avatar seated and supported, 2 complete synchronous supinated dumbbell curls using both elbows, hands palms up throughout, with cast immobile on separately supported padded platform. Full frame, no feet loading.' Video only unless verified Marcus audio exists.

### A2 — Seated dumbbell SHRUGS
- **Actual video:** Muscle & Strength seated shrug https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/seated-dumbbell-shrug.html ; secondary FITTR https://www.fittr.com/exercise-video/seated-dumbbell-shrugs-3413/
- **Start:** seated spine upright and stabilized, palms inward, weights hanging beside thighs, arms nearly straight.
- **Joint action:** ELEVATE BOTH SHOULDERS vertically toward ears as traps shorten, hold briefly, lower them vertically to starting position. Hands travel a few inches up/down as a consequence; elbows must not curl, arms must not abduct or press.
- **Reject:** arm curls, rolling shoulders forward/back, leaning, standing, cast/support movement.
- **Status:** generated 'Man performing shoulder shrugs' in Google Flow; verified working URL https://flow.google.com/project/882f7957-65ac-4160-95f5-d370dcd6e855/edit/405badfe-b01b-4298-91c9-60ee7ec6c55b ; **user technique approval pending**, not accepted for guided use.
- **Prompt essence:** 'Same original Flex still; two or three complete vertical shoulder-elevation/relaxation SHRUGS; dumbbell hand position at outer thighs; elbows remain long; no curling, no shoulder rolling. Entire cast on independent elevated cushion remains static. One wide fixed shot.'

### A3 — SEATED lateral raise (bilateral)
- **Sources:** user's reference FITTR https://www.fittr.com/exercise-video/seated-dumbbell-lateral-raises-29772/ ; Muscle & Strength seated specific https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/seated-dumbbell-lateral-raise.html ; ACE mechanics https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/26/lateral-raise/
- **Start:** palms facing inward, weights beside outer thighs, elbows softly and nearly FIXED flexed.
- **Joint action:** shoulder ABDUCTION to the **SIDES**. Both whole arms and weights travel laterally out and up to approximately shoulder level, creating wide 'T'. Reverse same sideways arcs completely to thighs.
- **Reject:** biceps curl (elbows fold), front raise (weights move ahead of torso), overhead press, shrug, 1-arm-only movement when bilateral required, partial rep, cast movement. Video sources sometimes show standing with weight on feet; ignore that part.
- **Status:** user approved corrected Flow sample https://flow.google.com/project/882f7957-65ac-4160-95f5-d370dcd6e855/edit/475bb83c-9c3c-4e4a-aad2-d3d03b6a2dd1 ; **still needs raw MP4 hosting / in-app embed**; older failed incorrect lateral video remains rejected.
- **Prompt essence:** 'Exactly same Flex, 2 visibly complete bilateral seated dumbbell LATERAL shoulder raises side-to-side in wide T arcs from outside thighs, no elbow flexion, full supported white LEFT cast in frame, no pressure through feet.'

### A5 / B5 — Seated arm-only boxing
- **References:** North Tees NHS seated punching https://www.nth.nhs.uk/resources/chair-exercises/ ; University of Iowa https://chtc.sites.uiowa.edu/resources/chair-exercises/chair-exercises-upper-body ; NHS Gloucestershire orthopaedic resource https://www.gloshospitals.nhs.uk/your-visit/patient-information-leaflets/simple-exercises-encourage-weight-loss/ ; demonstration https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xCady0pMGn0 (SilverSneakers gentle boxing).
- **Adaptation:** arms only, tightly supported seated trunk, shoulders steady, alternate small punches FORWARD to eye/chest height, elbow extends without hard locking; retract to light guard near cheeks. No trunk rotation or leg drive.
- **Reject:** visible punching powered by waist turn, shifting seat, pivoting either foot, jerky motion, leaning back, cast swinging. While safe for some groups, faster boxing/cardio may not be safe for THIS current injury; needs explicit orthopedist clearance before participation.
- **Prompt essence:** 'Approved Flex seated upright on stable supported bench, legs absolutely still and left cast elevated, alternate right-left short shadowboxing JABS, each fully extends and retracts, with no hips/trunk twisting and no feet involved, steady camera.'

## Exercise-by-exercise research — Equipment B

### B1 — Seated hammer curls
- **Actual video:** Muscle & Strength https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/seated-hammer-curl.html ; ACE https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/10/hammer-curl/
- **Start:** hex dumbbells at outer thighs, PALMS FACING INWARD at all times (neutral grip, thumbs point forward/up during curl), elbows next to torso.
- **Movement:** bend both elbows to bring weights near shoulders, slowly extend elbows back to thighs; NO forearm supination, shoulder swing or lateral raise.
- **Status:** video rendered/plays, technique approval pending https://flow.google.com/project/882f7957-65ac-4160-95f5-d370dcd6e855/edit/a125da34-b690-47c3-bb60-bdc7dc150f46
- **Prompt essence:** 'Exact original Flex seated; 2 complete neutral-grip HAMMER curls, palms always facing each other, upper arms pinned near torso, both casted and opposite leg still, cast separately elevated.'

### B2 — Seated dumbbell front raises
- **Specific seated video:** Muscle & Strength https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/seated-dumbbell-front-raise.html ; ACE front raise mechanics https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/54/front-raise/
- **Start:** hands holding photographed dumbbells IN FRONT of thighs, overhand/pronated grip or neutral variant explicitly chosen; elbows slight fixed bend.
- **Joint action:** shoulder flexion: whole arms lift IN FRONT of body toward shoulder height, then descend FRONT along same path, not sideways, no curls.
- **Reject:** lateral raise, shoulder press, extra shoulder shrug, upper-body swing, cast load. Require 2 distinguishable full reps.
- **Prompt essence:** 'Same approved Flex in cast-supported seated position, 2 complete bilateral FRONT arm raises, dumbbells from in front of thighs up in FORWARD vertical plane to shoulder height and back, nearly fixed elbows; no lateral motion or shoulder press.'

### B4 — Light seated dumbbell wrist curl
- **Videos:** Muscle & Strength seated wrist curl https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/dumbbell-wrist-curl.html ; verified Physio REHAB https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-MEIn6EgndI ; ACE flexion https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/30/wrist-curl-flexion/ ; ACE extension contrast https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/29/wrist-curl-extension/
- **Start:** FLEXION version, palm UP, forearm fully supported by armrest/table or bench not the injured thigh, elbow quiet; palm and hand extend slightly past support edge; one very light dumbbell. No kneeling or leg balance copied from source.
- **Movement:** hinge at **WRIST ONLY**, bending palm toward inside of forearm (flexion) then slowly lowering; elbow, upper arm and shoulder stationary.
- **Reject:** biceps curl, wrist extension version with palm DOWN (a DIFFERENT exercise), gripping with finger roll/dropping weight, cast/leg stabilization.
- **Prompt essence:** 'Original Flex seated with forearm on independent table, palm UP, one photographed light dumbbell in hand, do 2-3 complete small wrist-FLEXION arcs without moving elbow or shoulder, left white cast fully supported separately throughout.'

## Gentle program and rest — source-reviewed; do not waste four separate exercise video generations

G1 **Relaxed breathing:** supported sitting posture, no loaded movement, breath gently and normally. Source NHS https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress/ . Keep a simple coach cue/status screen, no mandatory video.
G2 **Open/close hands:** alternating finger flexion/extension or both hands open wide then gently make fist, upper arms quiet. Leeds NHS https://www.leedsth.nhs.uk/patients/resources/elbow-exercises/ . Keep a short illustrated cue or ONLY approved Flex if a video is ever requested.
G3 **Easy elbow bends:** elbow flexion/extension with forearms free and shoulders/torso quiet, no dumbbells. Leeds NHS https://www.leedsth.nhs.uk/patients/resources/elbow-exercises/ ; Newcastle NHS https://www.newcastle-hospitals.nhs.uk/services/newcastle-occupational-health-service/information-for-staff/physiotherapy/self-help-leaflets/1-minute-body-check/ . No need to spend Flow credits on generic motion.
G4 **Rest:** stationary supported position, breathing naturally. This is not an exercise requiring an AI video; keep a short rest screen.

The gentle cues still require consideration of medical restrictions/individual comfort; none imply clearance.
