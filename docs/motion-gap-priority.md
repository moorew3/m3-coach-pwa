## Exercise prompts

### dumbbellCurl
Standing bilateral dumbbell biceps curls. Start arms fully down with palms forward/supinated.
Curl both dumbbells toward shoulder height without swinging, elbows pinned near the torso, then
lower under control. Show at least two clear repetitions.

### chestPress
Machine chest press. Coach seated with back against pad, hands on chest-press handles at chest
height. Press handles forward to near full elbow extension, then return under control. Show at
least two clear repetitions. Must be a chest-press machine, not dumbbells or barbell.

### chestSupportedRow
Chest-supported row. Coach's chest remains supported against an incline/chest pad while pulling
two handles or dumbbells toward the ribcage, squeezing shoulder blades, then lowering under
control. Show two clear repetitions. Do not turn it into an unsupported bent-over row.

### legPress
45-degree or seated leg-press machine. Feet planted shoulder-width on platform, knees bend under
control, then coach presses platform away to near extension without locking knees. Show two clear
repetitions. Keep hips/back supported.

### rearDeltFly
Rear-delt fly. Coach hinges or sits chest-supported as appropriate, arms start in front/slightly
down, then sweep outward to the sides with soft elbows until upper arms align across the shoulders,
returning under control. Show two clear repetitions. Do not turn it into a row.

### easyWalk
Natural treadmill walk. Coach walks forward continuously on a treadmill at an easy recovery pace,
upright posture, arms swinging naturally. Show real alternating steps and continuous belt walking.

### externalRotation
Shoulder external rotation using a light resistance band/cable. Upper arm stays tucked to torso,
elbow bent about 90 degrees, forearm rotates outward then returns under control. Show multiple
clear repetitions. Do not abduct the shoulder or perform a row.

### shoulderPress
Standing or seated dumbbell shoulder press with two dumbbells. Start dumbbells at shoulder level,
press overhead to near full extension, lower under control. Show two clear repetitions. Do not use
a shoulder-press machine.

### trapBarDeadlift
Trap-bar deadlift with a visible hex/trap bar around the coach. Start standing inside the bar,
hinge/squat to grip neutral handles, stand tall by extending hips/knees, then lower under control.
Must use a true hex/trap bar, never a straight barbell.

### bulgarianSplitSquat
Bulgarian split squat. Rear foot elevated on bench, front foot planted. Lower rear knee toward floor
while front knee tracks over foot, then drive through front leg to stand. Show two controlled reps
on one side.

### farmerMarch
Farmer/suitcase march. Coach stands tall holding heavy dumbbells at sides and performs controlled
alternating high-knee marching steps while keeping torso upright and weights stable.

### battleRopeFinisher
Battle-rope finisher. Coach holds one battle-rope end in each hand and performs rapid alternating
rope waves from an athletic stance. Both ropes must visibly wave continuously.

### cablePunch
Standing cable punch. Cable anchored behind coach; one hand punches forward from guard against
cable resistance, then returns under control. Show repeated punches with stable stance and torso
rotation. Cable attachment must remain visible.

### medBallChestPass
Medicine-ball chest pass. Coach holds medicine ball at chest, forcefully passes it straight forward
from chest level, receives/retrieves it, and repeats. Show real ball travel; do not mime an empty-hand
press.

### reverseStepRow
Reverse step plus row combination. Coach steps one foot backward into a controlled reverse stance
while simultaneously pulling row handles/band toward ribs, then returns to standing and releases.
Show the coordinated step-and-row pattern.

### squatToCurl
Squat-to-curl combination with two dumbbells. Perform a controlled squat, rise to standing, then
perform a bilateral curl. Show the complete sequence clearly rather than blending it into one vague
movement.

### stepAltCurl
Step plus alternating curl. Coach performs continuous controlled steps while alternating one-arm
dumbbell curls, left then right. Preserve clear stepping and clear alternating curls.

### stepShoulderPress
Step plus shoulder press. Coach performs a controlled step while pressing dumbbells overhead,
returns/lower under control, then repeats. Both stepping and overhead press must be clearly visible.
